# Credits

## Practical bundles

The four practical bundles used in the demonstration are in `content/ar-activities`:
Setting Up a Computer, Respiratory System, Circulatory System and Human Eye. They are
provided for the development demonstration (RIDC) and its inspection, and are not
part of a production release.
